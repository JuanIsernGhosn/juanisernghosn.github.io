On **January 08, 2025**, **Canal Sur's Enred program** featured a special segment on the **AI-powered vandalism detection system** developed by **Innovasur** for the city of Granada. I had the honor of appearing alongside my colleague **Jonathan González** to explain how this cutting-edge system was built and deployed.

This project marks a significant milestone: **Granada became the first Spanish city to implement an early detection system for vandalism using Deep Learning**, one of the most advanced artificial intelligence techniques available today.

### 🤖 How the system works

The technology we developed leverages **real-time video analytics** and **computer vision** to detect chromatic changes on building facades and urban surfaces. When a change is detected, the system immediately sends an alert to the mobile devices of the Policía Local patrol operating in the area, providing:

- **Exact geolocation** of the graffiti
- **Precise timestamp** of when it occurred
- **Early warning capabilities** for suspicious behaviors like loitering or suspicious activity near urban infrastructure

This enables law enforcement to respond quickly and potentially catch perpetrators in the act.

### 🛠️ Technical implementation

Our system integrates seamlessly with Granada's existing **smart city infrastructure**, analyzing video feeds from strategically positioned cameras across the city. The AI models we developed can:

- Detect color changes on facades in real-time
- Distinguish between legitimate urban activity and vandalism
- Filter false positives to minimize unnecessary alerts
- Operate 24/7 with high accuracy

During the public presentation on **Calle Profesor José Cazorla**, we conducted a live demonstration where a graffiti artist created a test painting. The system successfully detected the change and immediately transmitted the information to the police tablets and central command, showcasing the technology's effectiveness.

---

### 💡 Impact and enforcement

This technological solution is part of a comprehensive strategy that includes:

**Strengthened sanctions:**
- **Very serious infractions** (graffiti exceeding €200 in cleaning costs, or containing xenophobic, sexist, or racist expressions): Up to **€3,000**
- **Serious infractions**: Up to **€2,000**
- **Graffiti on heritage sites** (BIC - Bien de Interés Cultural): Up to **€250,000** for serious cases

**Enhanced cleaning operations:**
- Special cleaning team with **35 operators**
- **109 facades and walls** already cleaned
- Ongoing interventions across emblematic locations, including Granada's iconic viewpoints
